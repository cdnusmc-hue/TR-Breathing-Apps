## 8. Interaction Notes

- Portrait mobile is primary; landscape remains usable.
- Essential child targets remain in the lower two-thirds of the screen.
- Stop and Get a Grown-up maintain consistent position.
- Reduced-motion screens preserve the same hierarchy with static art.
- Wireframes intentionally omit store, streak, score, social, and account elements.
