# Kids Breathing Coach — Interaction and Animation Storyboard

**Document ID:** KBC-SB-001  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Ready for clickable prototype

## 1. Golden Journey: Big Explorer Snake Breath

| Beat | Screen/action | Visual | Spoken/caption cue | Exit paths |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home selection | Suki curls around the Slow Down card | “Want a short settling pause?” | Other activity, Grown-up |
| 2 | Preview | Suki demonstrates one small rise and slow glide | “Watch one easy breath.” | Start, Something Else, Grown-up |
| 3 | Safety cue | Calm static frame | “Small, easy breaths. Stop anytime.” | Start, Back |
| 4 | Demonstration inhale | Suki's head rises slightly | “Breathe in comfortably.” | Stop, Grown-up |
| 5 | Demonstration exhale | Suki travels along an S-path | “Let it out with a soft sss.” | Stop, Grown-up |
| 6 | Participation | Four to six flexible cycles; trail fills | Short phase cues only | Pause, Stop, Something Else, Grown-up |
| 7 | Normal breath | Suki coils at rest | “Let your breathing do its own thing.” | Stop |
| 8 | Check-out | Suki rests; choices appear | “What do you notice?” | Same, Different, Not sure, Skip |
| 9 | Close | Pip lights path back Home | “You practiced. That is enough.” | Done, Another, Favorite |

## 2. Little Explorer Variation

- Preview demonstration is longer than the explanation.
- No numeric or time language.
- Three child cycles maximum before an optional **Again** choice.
- Instruction pair: **Small breath in** / **Sss goes out**.
- Adult participation prompt appears on first use.

## 3. Animal Motion Storyboards

### Bumblebee

Bee rests on flower → rises gently during inhale → follows a looping home path during soft hum → lands during normal breathing. The loop length follows the approved exhale window; animation does not stop abruptly if the child stops humming.

### Bear

Bear settles under blanket → blanket rises slightly → settles more slowly → stars dim between cycles. No breath hold or frozen “wait” phase. Moon Journey ends on a nearly static night scene.

### Flower and Candle

Flower approaches → petals open slightly during easy inhale → candle appears → flame bends and dances during gentle exhale. The flame is never extinguished, preventing a forceful success target.

### Bunny

Bunny notices one flower → makes one small sniff-like inhale → ears relax during ordinary exhale → returns to natural breathing. Two attempts maximum; no rapid sniff sequence.

### Tiger

Tiger stretches paws and shoulders → one easy inhale → soft `haa` while shoulders drop → offers Shake, Stretch, or Done. It sits in the alternative-tools branch.

## 4. Stop Storyboard

1. Child taps Stop.
2. Audio, haptic, and paced motion cease within 100 ms target.
3. Character returns to neutral pose without disappointment.
4. Message: **Okay. Let your breathing do its own thing.**
5. Choices: Home / Something Else / Get a Grown-up.

No confirmation dialog delays stopping.

## 5. Pause/Resume Storyboard

Pause immediately removes pacing and shows **Paused**. Resume never returns mid-inhale or mid-exhale; it begins with five seconds of normal breathing, then restarts at the next complete cycle.

## 6. Interruption Storyboard

Background, screen lock, navigation, phone call, or clock discontinuity ends the activity. On return: **That activity ended. Start again when you're ready.** No automatic catch-up or resume.

## 7. Low-Sensory Storyboard

Character remains static. A large shape and text card alternate between **Easy breath in**, **Let it out**, and **Normal breathing**. No music or haptics. Optional tone and narration remain separately selectable.
