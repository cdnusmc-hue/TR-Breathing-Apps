# Kids Breathing Coach — Six-Activity Script and Cadence Pack

**Document ID:** KBC-CONTENT-001  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Prototype content approved; clinical review pending

## Global Rules

- Timing is an animation window, not a requirement.
- No holds, maximal breaths, forceful exhale, accuracy score, or automatic pace escalation.
- Every activity begins with a demonstration and supports immediate Stop.
- Every close permits no change: **It is okay if you feel the same.**

## 1. Slow the Waves — Suki the Snake

**Purpose:** settling pause.  
**Mechanic:** controlled exhale.

Little: three cycles; 2–3 sec easy inhale, 3–4 sec soft hiss.  
Script: “Watch Suki.” → “Small breath in.” → “Sss goes out.” → “Let your breathing be.”

Big: four to six cycles; 3 sec inhale, 4–5 sec soft hiss.  
Script: “Breathe in comfortably.” → “Let it out with a soft sss.” → “No need to reach the end of the path.”

## 2. Tiny Reset — Clover the Bunny

**Purpose:** interrupt a busy moment without promising calm.  
**Mechanic:** single reset.

Little: normal breathing 5 sec; one 2-sec flower smell; one ordinary 3-sec exhale; repeat once only.  
Script: “Clover found a flower.” → “One tiny smell.” → “And let it go.”

Big: one or two cycles; comfortable 2–3 sec inhale and 3–4 sec exhale; then 10 sec normal breathing.  
Close: “Choose what comes next: move, senses, grown-up, or done.”

## 3. Try It With Me — Bibi the Bumblebee

**Purpose:** structured pause before something difficult.  
**Mechanic:** voiced exhale.

Little: three cycles; 2 sec inhale, 3 sec gentle hum.  
Script: “Bibi will show you.” → “Small breath in.” → “Hum home with Bibi.”

Big: four cycles; 3 sec inhale, 4–5 sec hum.  
Script: “Easy breath in.” → “Hum gently as Bibi flies home.” → “Now choose one small next step.”

## 4. Ready for the Next Thing — Daisy and Candle

**Purpose:** transition toward the next task.  
**Mechanic:** easy longer exhale.

Little: three cycles; smell 2 sec, candle dance 3 sec.  
Script: “Smell Daisy.” → “Make the flame dance.”

Big: four to six cycles; inhale 3 sec, exhale 4 sec.  
Script: “Breathe in like smelling a flower.” → “Let the air out gently enough to move the flame.” → “What is your next thing?”

## 5. Moon Journey — Bruno the Bear

**Purpose:** quiet bedtime transition.  
**Mechanic:** comfortable rise/fall with no holds.

Little: 90–120 sec story; paced cues only for first four cycles, then ambient normal breathing.  
Script: “Bruno is getting cozy.” → “The blanket rises.” → “The blanket settles.” → “Now breathing can do its own thing.”

Big: 3–4 min; six guided cycles at 3 sec in/4–5 sec out, followed by unpaced quiet narration.  
Close: dim **All done** state; no celebration or reflection prompt.

## 6. Buddy Breaths — Choose Together

**Purpose:** shared caregiver-child practice.  
**Mechanic:** adult and child select Snake, Bee, or Flower/Candle.

Little: adult watches one demonstration, then completes three cycles beside the child.  
Script: “Pick an animal together.” → “Watch first.” → “Now try side by side.”

Big: four to six cycles followed by optional prompt: “What would help next?”

Buddy Breaths never asks the adult to correct synchronization or breathing depth.

## Alternative Tool: Tavi the Tiger

Tavi is reached through **Something Else**. Stretch arms and shoulders, take one easy inhale, release one soft `haa`, then choose Shake / Stretch / Done. Maximum three repetitions. This is expressive movement, not a paced-breath intervention.

## Audio Direction

- Neutral warmth; no exaggerated baby voice.
- Little narration is slower but not whispering.
- Big narration avoids patronizing diminutives.
- Exhale sounds demonstrate softness, never duration competition.
- Background music ducks beneath every spoken cue and defaults off in low-sensory mode.

## Localization Notes

Animal sounds and metaphors require cultural/linguistic review. Do not assume `ssss`, humming, cow, or duck sounds are represented identically in every language. Timing and safety meaning must survive translation.
