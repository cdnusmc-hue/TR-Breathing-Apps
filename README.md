# TR Breathing Apps

Evidence-informed breathing experiences from Thinking Reimagined.

> Technology should evolve to serve humanity. Humanity should never be required to evolve to serve technology.

## Products

- **Kids / Quiet Creek** — child-friendly breathing guidance for ages 3–12.
- **Adult Breathing Coach** — adult wellness breathing experiences and supporting research.

## Current build focus

The active implementation lane is the Kids app's **Slow the Waves** vertical slice with Suki the Snake. Additional activities remain intentionally deferred until this slice passes family testing.

## Repository map

- `apps/kids/quiet-creek/` — installable Quiet Creek web app
- `docs/kids/` — Kids product, research, UX, content, and architecture records
- `docs/adult/` — Adult product records
- `docs/shared/` — portfolio-wide indexes and shared decisions
- `archive/` — retired material retained for traceability

## Safety and scope

These products are general wellness and educational tools, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Breathing should feel gentle and comfortable. Stop if an exercise causes dizziness, shortness of breath, discomfort, or distress.

## Status

Quiet Creek is a pre-release family-testing build. It is not yet a medical product or a production commercial release.
